The Story

One group. Every way a brand grows.

I'm Shaheen VP, Founder and Managing Director of the Adnora Group of Companies. Adnora began with stories: films and campaigns that make people stop scrolling. It grew into software, because a great brand also needs a website and an app that work as hard as its marketing. Today the group tells the story, builds the product, and, through Rentzo, helps people across Kerala find their next home.

  • 02

  • 03
